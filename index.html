Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Still respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Still app and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Still is designed to collect as little personal information as reasonably necessary to provide the service.

Still does not sell your personal information, use your information for third-party advertising, or track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

2. Sign in with Apple

Still requires Sign in with Apple before you can access the app.

Still does not request your name or email address. When you sign in, Still stores the Apple account identifier on your device and establishes a Supabase authentication session using Apple’s authorization token. This session lets Still securely associate account-only features, such as a Journey Gift, with the correct Still user.

Still does not use this information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Certain information related to your use of Still may be stored locally on your device. This may include saved content, listening history, playback progress, content and interface preferences, and temporary cached data.

This information is used to provide features such as saved journeys, listening history, playback continuity, and efficient content loading. Where such information remains only on your device, Still does not receive it.

4. Subscriptions and Purchases

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit services. Still does not directly collect or store your payment card or banking information.

The app may receive limited information from Apple necessary to determine whether a subscription or purchase is active, restore purchases, and provide access to purchased content. Apple's handling of purchase and payment information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

5. Content Delivery and Service Providers

Still uses Supabase infrastructure to provide authentication, Journey Gift access records, and certain remote content and related services, including audio and image resources.

The Still app does not intentionally collect or upload your precise location, advertising identifier, contacts, or similar device information for advertising purposes.

When the app communicates with online services, service providers and their network infrastructure may automatically process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot those services. This may include information such as IP addresses, request metadata, User-Agent information, and error or security logs.

Such technical processing is not used by Still for cross-app tracking or third-party advertising.

6. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Still does not currently integrate third-party advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs for behavioral advertising. Still does not use your information to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Still does not sell your personal information.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by Still may remain on your device until it is removed through the app, cleared by the operating system, or removed when the app is deleted, depending on the type of data.

You can use Delete Local Account Data in Still to remove Still's locally stored Apple authorization identifier, saved content, listening history, playback progress, and onboarding state from your device. This action does not delete or change your Apple account, cancel an active Apple subscription, or remove a Journey Gift that has already been assigned to your Still account.

Still does not sync your saved content, listening history, or playback progress to Supabase. You may contact us regarding privacy questions or a request to delete server-side Gift access records using the email address provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

Still is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Still in circumstances that require parental consent or deletion, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Still, our service providers, legal requirements, or privacy practices. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date above and make the revised policy available through Still or this website.
